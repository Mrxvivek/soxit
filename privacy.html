Data Governance & Trust

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

At SOXIT (including its operating divisions Consulting, Academia, and Open Media), we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy details the types of personal information we collect, how it is used and protected, and your rights under applicable data protection laws, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that is reasonably necessary for us to provide you with consulting services, academic programs, and media engagements:

  • Contact Enquiries: Full name, corporate email address, contact telephone number, organization, and project details provided via our enquiry form.
  • Career Applications: Name, contact details, curriculum vitae (CV), portfolio links, role preference, and background notes submitted through our careers portal.
  • Technical & Usage Telemetry: Anonymized technical metrics such as device type, browser platform, IP address, and interaction data used solely to optimize site performance and security.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is processed strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To respond to your business, consulting, or partnership requests.
  • To review and communicate regarding employment or internship applications.
  • To ensure platform security, verify system uptime, and prevent fraudulent submissions.
  • We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to external advertisers.

3. Data Retention & Security

We employ enterprise-grade transport encryption (TLS 1.3), access controls, and strict internal security protocols. Personal data from business enquiries is retained only for the duration necessary to service your request or fulfill regulatory obligations.

4. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of outdated or inaccurate information.
  • Withdraw your consent to data processing at any time.
  • Lodge an inquiry or grievance with our data protection team.
