What You Will Learn

1. ITSM Fundamentals

Understand the principles of managing and delivering effective IT services.

2. Service Desk

Explore how service desks support users, manage requests, and coordinate IT support.

3. Incident Management

Understand how IT incidents can be recorded, prioritized, tracked, and resolved.

4. Problem Management

Learn the difference between incidents and problems and understand structured problem-management concepts.

Understand how common user service requests can be organized and handled efficiently.

Learn how feedback, performance information, and lessons learned can support ongoing service improvement.

IT Service Workflow

USER REQUEST
User submits an inquiry, incident report, or service request via support channels.
↓
LOG TICKET
Service desk registers the request details, contact info, and initial timestamp.
↓
CLASSIFY
Categorize the issue by type, impacted component, or service category.
↓
PRIORITIZE
Evaluate urgency and business impact to establish ticket priority and SLA targets.
↓
ASSIGN
Route ticket to the appropriate support tier, specialist, or fulfillment group.
↓
RESOLVE
Apply technical fix, workarounds, or fulfill user service request.
↓
VERIFY
Confirm service restoration and user satisfaction with the provided solution.
↓
CLOSE
Record resolution notes, update knowledge assets, and mark ticket completed.
↓
REVIEW
Analyze resolution performance data to feed continual service improvement plans.
