What does this role do?

Backend developers build and maintain the invisible engine behind web and mobile applications. They translate business requirements into fault-tolerant services, design normalized and high-throughput data stores, enforce strict authentication boundaries, and ensure low-latency performance under heavy traffic spikes.

Architects multi-region distributed systems, event-driven microservices, zero-downtime data migrations, and SLO observability.
