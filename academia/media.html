What You Will Learn

1. Media Production Fundamentals

Understand the fundamentals of digital media production, creative workflows, and content planning.

2. Visual Storytelling

Learn how ideas can be communicated through visuals, composition, sequencing, and storytelling.

3. Video Production

Explore the basic concepts of video creation, production planning, framing, and visual composition.

4. Video Editing

Understand editing workflows, timelines, transitions, sequencing, and content refinement.

5. Graphic Design

Explore foundational visual-design concepts for creating digital graphics and media assets.

6. Audio & Sound

Understand basic audio concepts, recording workflows, sound editing, and audio quality.

7. Motion & Visual Effects

Explore foundational concepts of motion graphics, visual effects, and animated digital content.

Apply media-production concepts through a practical creative project.
